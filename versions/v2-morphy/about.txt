NAME
v2-morphy
NAMESAKE
Named after Morphy because it is the first build that develops its search quickly and sees tactics soundly.
DATE
2026-09-02 (Archived 2026-09-03)
WHAT IT IS
A significantly stronger engine with a transposition table, MVV-LVA and killer/history move ordering, and quiescence search. Includes three unplanned speedups, most notably the transposition key change which was worth a 20-25% node rate bump.
CHANGED FROM
v1-philidor; added TT, ordered moves by MVV-LVA/killers/history, and implemented delta-pruned quiescence search. Unplanned speedups: numba removal, evaluation loops flattened into precomputed tables, and polyglot zobrist_hash replaced with native board._transposition_key().
MEASURED
vs baselines/greedy (fast arena 10s + 0.1s): 100.0% (+20 =0 -0)
vs Stockfish depth 2 (30s + 0.3s): 100.0% (+10 =0 -0)
typical completed depth: 4 to 5 (depending on position)
selective depth: up to 24 plies
KNOWN WEAKNESSES
Move ordering is still somewhat weak (only ~55% first-move beta cutoffs), leading to a high node count in qsearch (68%). No null move pruning or late move reductions (search tree grows too fast), and no endgame knowledge. Subject to threefold repetition blindness.
